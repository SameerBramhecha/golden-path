# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Golden Path a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes being respectful, constructive, open to feedback, and focused on the project's best interests.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
